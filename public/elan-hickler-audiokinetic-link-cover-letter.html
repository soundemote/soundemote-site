Hello Audiokinetic team,

I am applying because Wwise sits directly in the area I want to contribute to: game audio technology, audio engine integration, DSP, acoustics, creative tools, and sound-designer workflow.

I studied Wwise in college while earning my BA in Audio for Visual Media, and it became one of the sparks that pushed me toward building original audio-engine and audiovisual-tool systems for games and interactive media. I am not only interested in operating existing middleware; I am interested in understanding and building the systems underneath that make interactive sound possible.

I founded Soundemote and am building a work-in-progress analog modelled audiovisual engine for audio tools, DSP prototyping, node graphs, visual response, and eventual desktop/audio-plugin direction. My background combines audio software, sound design, composition, C++, JavaScript, Python, shader work, workflow tooling, and thousands of hours of detailed audio editing.

The most direct proof of my work is this DSP / audiovisual engine demo: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PpcdN-DXYdc. I built the audio DSP, visual interface programming, analog modelled oscilloscope behavior, analog modelled synthesis/signal-processing systems, and musical arrangement shown in the demo. The audiovisual engine, interface, arrangement, and synthesized sound are my work; the only external sound sources are the percussion samples.

I would be excited to contribute to Audiokinetic as someone who understands both the technical and creative sides of audio tools: how they sound, how they behave, how they are tested, and how they feel to use in real production work.
