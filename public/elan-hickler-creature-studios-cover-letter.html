Cover Letter

Hello crea-ture Studios team,

I am applying for the Technical Artist role because your posting describes exactly the kind of cross-disciplinary work I care about: building tools and workflows, bridging art/design/programming, prototyping shaders and rendering techniques, solving technical constraints, and helping a small team make PC/console original IP in a different way.

My background is unusual but directly relevant: I founded Soundemote and build creative audio/visual software, DSP systems, shader-driven interfaces, node-style tools, visualizers, and workflow prototypes. I also have hands-on game-oriented art experience with modeling, character/body sculpting, clothing, procedural texturing, shape keys, bone animation, cloth simulation, and soft-body workflows.

I studied Wwise and game-audio implementation in college while earning my BA in Audio for Visual Media. That experience helped inspire my long-term goal of building original engine-style systems for games and interactive media: tools where sound, visuals, shaders, controls, and creative workflow can communicate cleanly instead of living in separate silos.

Main proof of work: DSP / audiovisual engine demo

For this demo, I built the audio DSP behind the synthesizers used in the music: analog emulative filters and delay/reverb. I built the visual modular interface, analog modelled oscilloscope behavior, and analog modelled synthesis/signal-processing systems. The audiovisual engine, interface, arrangement, and synthesized sound are my work; the only external source is the percussion samples.

I would be excited to contribute to crea-ture Studios as a technical artist with strong creative-tool instincts: someone who can prototype, test, document, optimize, communicate across disciplines, and help artists/designers/programmers move faster without losing the creative spark.

Thank you for considering my application.

Best,
Elan Hickler
