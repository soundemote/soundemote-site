Cover Letter

I am applying for the Audio Lead (Games) role. My background is in practical game-oriented audio production: DSP implementation, audio tool design, interactive workflows, and music and sound systems.

I bring long-term experience in composing and engineering electronic sound systems, plus hands-on work building real-time, analog-modeled, audiovisual tools where audio and visuals are tightly coupled. I also have direct experience with game-audio implementation workflows, including Wwise and Unreal integration patterns learned in formal study. I can contribute at both prototype and production depth: from efficient low level dsp math and architecture to end user UI/UX impementation.

I founded Soundemote and developed a custom DSP and visual workflow stack that focuses on reliability, responsiveness, and artist-level control without overcomplicating interfaces. That work includes analog-emulative synthesis, modular interaction logic, and performance-conscious UI and sequencing behavior for creative users under production constraints.

Main proof of work: DSP / audiovisual engine demo

For this demo, I built the audio DSP behind the synthesizers used in the music: analog emulative sound generators, filters, and delay/reverb. I built the visual modular interface, analog modelled oscilloscope behavior, and analog modelled synthesis/signal-processing systems. The audiovisual engine, interface, arrangement, and synthesized sound are my work; the only external source is the percussion samples.

I am able to contribute as a systems-first builder: strong at translating sonic goals into playable implementations, keeping iteration fast, and maintaining technical quality across composition, middleware integration, and runtime behavior. My approach is straightforward: clear signal path, low friction handoff, and measurable improvements to production output.

Thank you for your consideration,

Elan Hickler
