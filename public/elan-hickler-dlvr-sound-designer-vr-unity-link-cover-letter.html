Cover Letter

Hello DLVR team,

I am applying for the Sound Designer, VR/Unity role.

My background is game-oriented audio, DSP, sound design, synthesis, music systems, and end-user UI/UX implementation. I build audio systems that respond to controls, timing, visuals, and performance constraints, with a focus on analog modelling and physical accuracy.

I founded Soundemote and build analog-modelled audiovisual software: DSP systems, synthesis tools, shader-driven interfaces, visualizers, node-style workflows, and real-time control systems. My background also includes thousands of hours of detailed audio editing, music composition, sound design, audio synthesis/resynthesis, and game-oriented visual work.

This role is interesting to me because I see virtual reality as a big part of the future. I want to design the next generation spatial audio, Unity implementation, routing, snapshots, ducking, parameter control, while considering hardware/memory/CPU constraints. I can iterate fast and will work well with a small or large team.

I studied Wwise and game-audio implementation in college while earning my BA in Audio for Visual Media. That experience inspired my long-term goal to build my own audio engine for games, audio plugins, and audiovisual production where sound, controls, gameplay, visuals, music, and workflow communicate cleanly and efficiently.

Main proof of work: DSP / audiovisual engine demo

For this demo, I built the audio DSP behind the synthesizers used in the music: analog emulative sound generators and filters, and delay/reverb. I built the visual modular interface, analog modelled oscilloscope behavior, and analog modelled synthesis/signal-processing systems. The audiovisual engine, interface, arrangement, and synthesized sound are my work; the only external source is the percussion samples.

I can contribute as a practical sound designer and technical audio builder: creating original sounds with pure math, implementing them cleanly into an engine, keeping performance in view, and making systems that are easy to tune as gameplay changes.

Thank you for considering my application,
Elan Hickler
