Soundemote Sandbox is an audiovisual workstation for building instruments, effects, visualizers, and educational learning devices. Sound and image are equal creative partners: music sequencing, visual sequencing, shader instruments, camera motion, modulation, and export belong inside one low-friction creative workflow.

The idea starts with the appeal of a pretty oscilloscope and expands it into the whole workstation: the controls, graphs, meters, patch cables, oscilloscopes, and exports should be useful enough for real work and beautiful enough to become part of the artwork.

Sound and image are generated by inspectable algorithms, most of which can be explored inside the on-screen manual. The manual is part lab notebook, part instrument guide, and part map of the mathematics behind the work.

The control language is inspired by analog modular instruments: 0.1v/octave oscillators, multi-waveform audio outputs, reset inputs, and vactrol-style light-controlled modules with LEDs that make the signal feel visible as well as audible.

Imagine routing a module back into itself and it just works, then seeing the effect of chaotic feedback.

Patch an oscillator into a filter, watch the waveform bend, drive a shader from the same signal, and export the audiovisual moment without leaving the workspace.

The Core Idea

Not a DAW with video bolted on. Not a VJ tool with audio tacked on. An Audio Visual Workstation where music and visuals are composed together from the first beat, with analog character treated as part of the instrument instead of a decoration.

Analog Beauty

Analog-modeled modules, oscilloscope phosphor behavior, glowing signal paths, and a calm interface are built together so the workstation feels alive, readable, and inviting. LEDs, reset inputs, multi-waveform outputs, and light-controlled behavior are part of the visual language.

what it is not

  • Not a DAW with video bolted on.
  • Not a VJ tool with audio tacked on.
  • Not a node-based programming environment that asks the artist to build the instrument first.
  • Not another split workflow where music, visuals, cameras, and export live in separate mental models.

what it is

  • An Audio Visual Workstation where sound and image are equal creative partners.
  • A visual modular audio workspace for native music systems, visual systems, shader instruments, and audio-reactive visuals.
  • A music-first creative surface where video, shaders, cameras, and motion can be composed like instruments.
  • A low-friction workflow for making music you can see.

composition model

  • Every beat can have a sound, an image, a motion, and a light.
  • Audio, video, shaders, cameras, modulation, and export belong to the same composition.
  • Visuals are not an add-on, preview track, plug-in hack, or separate performance layer.
  • The system starts with sound and continues into energy, motion, and image.

who it opens doors for

One workstation, many ways to learn and publish

For Teachers

Patches can be sequenced like lesson plans. A class can move from oscillator basics to harmonics, additive synthesis, envelopes, filters, timing, video signals, shader parameters, and mathematical motion while hearing and seeing each concept unfold.

For Kids

Made to be easy to use, sonically interesting, and visually alive. Ear protection and speaker protection are built in, and the audio and video are generated by inspectable algorithms, not by AI audio or video generators.

For Mathematicians

Soundemote can become a living math surface: waves, attractors, modulation, feedback, curves, timing, phase, geometry, and signal flow made audible, visible, and explorable inside the same instrument. Even controls are calculators, such as typing 220 / 2 into a slider and getting 110.

For Social Media Creators

Direct-to-audio and direct-to-video buttons should make sharing feel immediate. Make a beat, watch it move, customize the UI, change the oscilloscope look and feel, and export the audiovisual moment without fighting a separate editor.

For Sample Library Creators

The engine can support advanced infrastructure for one-button multilayer sample library creation: generate round robins, velocity layers, organize exports with metadata, and turn beautiful DSP systems into reusable creative sample packs.

For Plugin Developers

Built from the ground up for audio plugin development, with CLAP hosting, metaparameters, mixed and manipulated multipoint graphs, in-out-in feedback routing, and anything-to-anything modulation, including audio modulating video and planned video modulating audio.

For VJs And Shader Artists

Shader code editing, audio-controlled shader parameters, camera motion, and screen behavior belong beside the sound. Hear the hit and see the screen shake at the same time, because both events come from one composition.
